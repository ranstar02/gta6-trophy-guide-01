    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, in the order to play them. Built from the official trophy descriptions: which story chapters and missions come first, which collectibles and counts come next, and what to clean up last. Expect around 200 hours in total — there are no online trophies and no difficulty trophies, so nothing here is hard, it just takes a while.

    Before anything else: turn on manual saves. Keep one save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission — the game has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. And never use a cheat: cheats switch trophies off.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy on the whole list can be missed. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam, at your own pace.

        How to stay safe

        • Meditate at the Chapter 1 spot before finishing Chapter 1.
        • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
        • Do not rely on mission replay — there is none.
