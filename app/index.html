    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions: story chapters and missions first, then the 100% checklist, then clean-up. PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and about 200 hours; Push Square says 250 hours or more. No trophy needs online play, and there is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the set is reported as missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free roam, and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable.

        How to stay safe

        • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.
        • Keep a second save from before the final mission.
        • Keep cheats off for the whole run: cheats switch trophies and saving off.
        • Optional missions (black icons with pink letters) disappear after a while, but they do not block any trophy or the 100% checklist.
